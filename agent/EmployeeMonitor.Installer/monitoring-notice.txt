COMPANY MONITORING NOTICE

This software is installed on a company-owned device and performs transparent monitoring in accordance with company
policy. A system-tray indicator shows when monitoring is active and lists exactly what is monitored.

What may be monitored (each category is controlled by company policy and can be turned off):
  - Work session: sign-in / sign-out, active / idle, lock / unlock
  - Foreground application usage
  - Website domains visited (domain names only)
  - USB device connect / disconnect events
  - Network connection status
  - Zscaler connection status
  - Periodic screenshots
  - Authorized, logged, time-limited live screen view by an administrator

What is NEVER captured:
  - Keystrokes or anything you type
  - Passwords, credentials, cookies or authentication tokens
  - The contents of private messages, documents or web pages
  - Webcam or microphone
  - The contents of files on USB devices

Monitoring is not hidden and can be uninstalled by IT administrators. If you have questions, contact your IT
department.
